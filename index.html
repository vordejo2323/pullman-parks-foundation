FIRST PROJECT · SPRING 2027

Keep the Teen Center open

The Teen Center is a free program at the Pullman Rec Center for Lincoln Middle School students, every school day from 3:30 to 5:00. Its grant ends in December, and City budget cuts mean Parks and Recreation can no longer cover it. $6,897 keeps it running from January 4 to June 10.

$0 raisedof the $6,897 spring goal

Fall 2023

More than 50 middle schoolers in the gym every afternoon with nothing structured to do, and behavior problems Rec Center staff and LMS principals were struggling to manage.

Fall 2026

39 students registered and 10 to 15 every day, with a coordinator running cooking, guest speakers, movies, and gym time.

ABOUT

Neighbors backing the places we share

The Pullman Parks and Recreation Foundation is a volunteer nonprofit formed in 2026 to raise private money for parks, recreation facilities, and recreation programs serving Pullman and the Palouse. We are independent of the City of Pullman and grant what we raise to support its parks and recreation work.
