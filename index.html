Keep the Teen Center open

FOR FUNDERS

What sponsors usually ask

Quick answers for banks, credit unions, and businesses reviewing a request.

Who receives the money?

Gifts go to the Pullman Parks and Recreation Foundation, not the City. The Foundation then makes a grant to the City of Pullman restricted to the Teen Center, so the money stays with the program. If our IRS approval has not arrived before payment is due, gifts will be received through a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor instead, and we will confirm that with you first.

What is the Foundation's legal status?

The Pullman Parks and Recreation Foundation is a Washington nonprofit corporation incorporated in October 2026 (UBI 606 314 698, EIN 43 2682645). Our application for recognition as a 501(c)(3) public charity was filed with the IRS on October 28, 2026 and is pending.

Is my gift tax deductible?

Once the IRS grants 501(c)(3) status, recognition generally applies back to the date of incorporation, so gifts made to the Foundation would qualify. Gifts made through a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor also qualify. Every donor receives a written acknowledgment. Please check with your tax advisor about your situation.

Who does the program serve?

Lincoln Middle School students in Pullman, Whitman County, Washington. 39 students are registered this fall and 10 to 15 attend every school day. The program is free to every family.

Is the program open to everyone?

Yes. The Teen Center serves any Lincoln Middle School student without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, sexual orientation, or gender identity. No religious participation is required, and it is not a political or partisan activity.

How will the money be used?

All of it goes to the program: $5,697 for the coordinator and $1,200 for snacks and activity supplies, covering about 104 afternoons from January 4 to June 10, 2027. No portion goes to Foundation overhead.

What does the program do?

A trusted adult coordinator runs structured afternoons from 3:30 to 5:00 with cooking, guest speakers, movies, and gym time. Before the program began in 2024, more than 50 middle schoolers were in the gym each afternoon with no structure, and both Rec Center staff and LMS principals were managing the behavior that followed.

How will sponsors hear about results?

Each sponsor receives a short report in June with attendance numbers and a breakdown of spending. Sponsors are thanked publicly unless they prefer to stay anonymous.

What is the deadline?

Pledges need to be committed by December 15 so the City can confirm the spring schedule. Payment is due by January 7, 2027. The program opens January 4.

Neighbors backing the places we share

We are independent of the City of Pullman and grant what we raise to support its parks and recreation work.
